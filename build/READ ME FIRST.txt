GAME SHOW STUDIO  —  v1.0
Trivia Blitz  •  Quiz Board  •  Spin & Solve
==================================================

INSTALL (one time, about 2 minutes)
--------------------------------------------------
1. Right-click the .zip file you downloaded and choose "Extract All...".
   (Don't run it from inside the zip. It has to be extracted first.)
2. Move the extracted "Game Show Studio" folder somewhere permanent,
   for example your Documents folder or C:\Games.
3. Open the folder and double-click "Game Show Studio.exe".
4. The first time only, Windows may show a blue box saying
   "Windows protected your PC". This happens because the app is new
   and not "signed" by a big publisher. Click "More info", then
   "Run anyway".
5. Optional: right-click "Game Show Studio.exe" > "Show more options" >
   "Send to" > "Desktop (create shortcut)" so it's one click next time.

RUNNING A GAME NIGHT
--------------------------------------------------
- Connect the laptop to the projector or TV, then press F11 for full screen.
- Teams & Scores: pick 2–10 teams and name them. Scores carry across
  all three games, and you can fix any score with the + / − buttons.
- Press Space to move things along (reveal, next, spin).
- "How to play" on the home screen explains each game.

ADDING YOUR OWN QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------
Option A: Home > Question Manager > "+ Add".
Option B, for lots of questions at once:
  1. Open a file from the "Question Templates" folder in Notepad.
  2. Replace the examples with your questions and save.
  3. Question Manager > pick the right tab > "Import from text file".
     The app tells you exactly which lines (if any) need fixing.
Each template explains its format at the top.

YOUR QUESTIONS ARE SAVED AUTOMATICALLY in:
  %APPDATA%\Game Show Studio\questions.json
Use "Back up everything" in the Question Manager to save a copy
(to a USB stick, for example), and "Restore backup" on another
computer to bring everything over.

UPDATING TO A NEW VERSION
--------------------------------------------------
Delete the old "Game Show Studio" folder and extract the new one.
Your questions and teams are stored separately, so they stay put.
